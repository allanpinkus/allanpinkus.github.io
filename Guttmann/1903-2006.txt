

Here is the verbatim English translation of the Hungarian notary document (written partly in Hungarian and partly in German):

Page 1

Case No.: 2006 / 1903.   Registry No.: 16960 / Gerhard   Notarized Document.   (1 Krona revenue stamp)   

I, Dr. Lajos Dombi, acting as the officially authorized deputy standing in place of Dr. Ferencz Stamberger, Royal Notary of Budapest, 
on this day in my office located in Budapest, District V, Váradi-utca No. 2, appeared before me: Izsák Guttmann, contractor, 
residing at District V, Váradi-utca No. 2, Budapest; as well as the following parties:   Izsák Guttmann, 
resident at District V, Váradi-utca No. 2, Budapest,   Lipót Frank, resident of Újpest,   
Alfréd Guttmann, resident at District IV, Andrássy-út No. 14, Budapest, and   Hermann Guttmann, resident at 
District IV, Alsó-erdőhegy-utca No. 4, Budapest, all contractors, whose personal identities were certified 
by Dr. Lázár Sámuel, attorney-at-law, residing at District IV, Andrássy-út No. 2, Budapest, and Dr. Ármin Fekete, 
attorney-at-law, residing at District III, Korona...   

Page 2...-herceg utca No. 17, Budapest, who are personally known to me, and requested that the following 
agreement be recorded into a notary document in the German language:   

Dissolution Contract and Declaration.   Concluded between Messrs. Izsák Guttmann, Leopold Frank, Alfred Guttmann, and Hermann Guttmann, 
respectively, as follows:   

Messrs. Izsák Guttmann and Leopold Frank entered into an agreement on May 27, 1894, according to which all earthwork construction 
enterprises and deliveries for the Danube and for the year 1894 were to be conducted jointly and on joint account, whereby profit 
and loss were to be shared in the proportion of $66\frac{2}{3}\%$ [sixty-six and two-thirds percent] to Mr. Izsák Guttmann, and 
$33\frac{1}{3}\%$ [thirty-three and one-third percent] to Mr. Leopold Frank.   

Page 3 In consideration of the fact that regarding the Kácsér canal construction between the parties involved here, as a 
special agreement was made, Mr. Izsák Guttmann with regard to this situation — and as he could no longer participate actively 
in the business due to advanced age — Mr. Izsák Guttmann, with the consent of the involved parties, has retired from the present 
special business relationship, and the following mutual conditions have been agreed upon:   

1. The business relationship concluded between Messrs. Izsák Guttmann and Leopold Frank on May 27, 1894 — and with the 
co-participation of Alfred Guttmann and Hermann Guttmann still in force today — is, with the consent of the 
undersigned parties, dissolved as of today, and all present and future undertakings...   

Page 4...and deliveries are taken over by Messrs. Leopold Frank, Alfred Guttmann, and Hermann Guttmann, who form a public 
trading company under the firm name "Guttmann, Frank", and carry out this business management under their own responsibility, 
obligating themselves to do so.   The present settlement regarding the existing business relations takes place as follows:   
That, on the basis of the balance sheet drawn up for this purpose, a further takeover takes place, and a total sum of liquidated, 
mutual claims derived therefrom has been settled, so that all amounts due to Mr. Izsák Guttmann, or which the company has to 
pay to Mr. Izsák Guttmann, are mutually settled up to the end of the current year.   

2. Regarding the preparation...   (Stamp / Attestation of Deputy Notary Dr. Lajos Dombi at the bottom)   

Page 5 (1 Krona stamp dated 1898)   ...of the final balance sheet, or final settlement, it is mutually agreed that the 
management of the enterprise shall pass to the new company at purchase value. It is further agreed that Messrs. Leopold Frank, 
Alfred Guttmann, and Hermann Guttmann, or Guttmann & Frank, shall submit a final settlement within twenty-five (25) days, 
and in the event that Mr. Izsák Guttmann does not object to this final settlement within fifty (50) days from the day of 
delivery of this statement, this accounting shall be considered final and legally binding...   

3. In the event of any disputes, all parties to the contract submit themselves to the jurisdiction of the Court 
of Arbitration of the local Goods and Stock Exchange.   

Page 6  Whereupon, this notary document having been recorded, read aloud in the presence of the parties, and 
fully approved by them as reflecting their exact intentions, was signed by them by hand.   

Done at Budapest, this twenty-fifth (25th) day of August in the year 1903 (nineteen hundred and three).   

Fee Register:   Work fee: 100 K   Writing fee: 2 K 40 h   Stamp: 2 K   Total: 104 K 40 h

(Notarial signature and official seal):   Dr. Lajos Dombi, as authorized deputy for Dr. Ferencz Stamberger, Royal Notary of Budapest.
