Pavle A. Gutman

TRADE-INDUSTRIAL CHAMBER IN NOVI SAD

Reg. number: 2 / 1945 Jun 10 1945
Novi Sad

MASTER INDEX CARD OF THE SHOP — ENTERPRISE OWNER

Surname, first letter of father's name, and baptismal name: Gutman A. Pavle   
Owner / co-owner / manager of the firm: Gutman i Frank gradjevinsko i brodarsko preduzeće, Novi Sad   
Born in the place of: Budapest, municipality: Budapest, district: Budapest, state: Hungary   
Day, month, and year of birth: September 29, 1902   
Nationality: Jewish   
Citizenship: Yugoslav   
Religion: Mosaic   
   
Schooling and professional qualifications:
         a) Completed general schooling: primary school, 8 grades of secondary school with matura, university faculty in Vienna (Wien).   
         b) Professional qualifications: Completed gymnasium with matura in Budapest, and Abiturient course in Vienna (Hochschule für Welthandel, Wien).   
        
Became owner / co-owner for the first time in the year: March 7, 1936, in the place of Novi Sad, specifically of the 
      enterprise (state firm): Gutman i Frank, Novi Sad.   
    
Previous occupation: "After schooling, worked as a volunteer and later as an employee at a shipping company in Hamburg, 
        and from there entered in the year 1923 first as an employee and later as a co-owner into the firm. He kept the 
		books and managed the commercial department of the firm. After the entry of the occupier, he was called up for 
		forced labor, and according to a Red Cross report, he went missing on January 21, 1943, near Nikolayevska 
		close to Ostrogozhsk on the Russian front."   
   
Note at bottom: "Since Gutman A. Pavle has not returned to this day from forced labor, we have compiled a brief biography for him."   
    
Date: June 9, 1945   
Place: Novi Sad   
Signed Mirko Gutman, Rudof Gutman
