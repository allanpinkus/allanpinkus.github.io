
Mirko A. Gutman

TRADE-INDUSTRIAL CHAMBER IN NOVI SAD

Reg. number: 2 / 1945 Jun 10 1945

Novi Sad

MASTER INDEX CARD OF THE SHOP — ENTERPRISE OWNER

Surname, first letter of father's name, and baptismal name: Gutman A. Mirko /Imre/   
Owner / co-owner / manager of the firm: Gutman i Frank gradjevinsko i brodarsko preduzeće, Novi Sad   
Born in the place of: Budapest, municipality: Budapest, district: Budapest, state: Hungary   
Day, month, and year of birth: March 2, 1901   
Nationality: Jewish   
Citizenship: Yugoslav   
Religion: Mosaic   
   
Schooling and professional qualifications:
 a) Completed general schooling: primary school, 8 grades of secondary school with matura, university faculty at the Technical University in Vienna.   
 b) Professional qualifications: Secondary real school completed with matura in Budapest, and Technical University 
    for Civil Engineering in Vienna (Technische Hochschule Bauingenieurfach - Wien).   
	
[From Gemini: He studied Civil Engineering (Bauingenieurwesen), covering structural engineering, geotechnics, transport, hydraulics, 
and building mechanics. This was one of the premier engineering schools in Central and Eastern Europe, training top engineers. AP]
	
       
Became owner / co-owner for the first time in the year: March 7, 1936, in the place of Novi Sad, 
     specifically of the enterprise (state firm): Gutman i Frank, gradjevinsko i brodarsko preduzeće, Novi Sad.   
    
Previous occupation: "After completing secondary real school and matura in Budapest, I finished a private commercial school for 
     bookkeeping and commercial correspondence, and after that I entered into service at 'Magyar Leszámítoló és Pénzváltó Bank 
	 Berlini téri fiók, Budapest', where I was in service for one year, after which I was an employee of the shipping/forwarding 
	 firm 'Schenker & Co'. From this firm I resigned and came as a sailor to Bavarian Lloyd, where I traveled on motor 
	 boat M.II. and tugboat D.IX. Later I entered as an employee at the timber company 'Danubia d.d. in Novi Sad'. After 
	 that commercial and technical preparation, I traveled to Vienna and finished the Technical University where I received 
	 a diploma in the civil engineering branch. As a young employed engineer of the construction enterprise 'Progres' Sarajevo, 
	 I managed construction at the Airport in Skoplje and built a school by myself.  In the year 1929, I came to our enterprise 
	 and worked there with interruptions. Among other works, as a partner of the firm 'Rasina' I built the Požarevac–Kučevo railway 
	 line, III section, and in the partnership 'Ing. Daka Popović and Gutman i Frank' the embankment between Novi Sad and Kać. 
	 In my own firm, previously as an employee and later as a co-owner, I completed the technical management of the enterprise 
	 as an authorized engineer, and as such I managed quarries in Dobra, Krčedin, Golubac, and Batina, waterworks on the Danube 
	 and its tributaries, and I also built roads, sewage systems, and other structures at the Airport in Novi Sad. I supervised 
	 floating vessels and managed the shipyard. On April 4, 1941, I was called up to the Engineers Barracks in Niš and from that 
	 time I stopped working at the firm, because after coming home on July 14, 1941, the occupier, Hungarian authorities, appointed 
	 a commissioner for our firm, who threw us owners out of our enterprise. Now I have returned from German deportation, and 
	 I wish for the enterprise to continue its work, in the first place for securing the riverbank from flooding, etc."   
    
[From Gemini: The Magyar Leszámítoló és Pénzváltó Bank (Hungarian Discount and Exchange Bank) was one of the largest 
and most influential commercial banks in Hungary during the late 19th and early 20th centuries. He worked at the bank's branch 
office located at Berlin Square in Budapest (Berlini téri fiók). "Schenker & Co." was one of Central Europe's prominent 
international transport and logistics firms (it went through numerous changes andt remains a massive global freight 
company today operating as DB Schenker). He worked for Bayerischer Lloyd (Bavarian Lloyd), which was a major shipping 
line on the Danube River. Boat M. II. was a motorized cargo vessel. Tugboat D.IX was a steam powered tugboat that
towed large, unpowered barges in long lines up and down the Danube. 'Danubia d.d." was a timber and wood-processing enterprise 
operating in northern Serbia (the Bačka / Vojvodina region) along the Danube River during the interwar years. The "Airport" 
mentioned was in Skopje, now Northern Macedonia. David "Daka" Popović (1886–1967) was a prominent Serbian civil engineer, 
architect, publisher, and high-ranking political figure. He worked on large infrastructure projects, often jointly with firms
such as "Gutman i Frank". Dobra, Krcedin, Golubac and Batina are all place along the Danube. AP]
