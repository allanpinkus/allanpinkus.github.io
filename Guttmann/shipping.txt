


CERTIFICATE / PERMIT in the sense of § 15 of the agreement on navigation on the Danube, which acquired legal force 
by order of 7 November 1857, or under Article XVI of 1867 introduced among the laws of the state and valid for the 
territory of the Hungarian crown, and under § IX and VII, or under Law No. XI § II of 1870 of the customs and 
commercial alliance treaty, is issued to GUTTMANN & FRANK, residents of Novi Sad, hydraulic engineering contractors, 
a PERMIT for steam navigation under the following conditions:   

§ 1 The above-named are permitted to maintain a steam vessel operation for their hydraulic engineering 
enterprise on the Danube and its tributaries.   

§ 2 The named license holder is required to strictly abide by the provisions of this permit, and besides that particularly:   
a) to abide by the provisions of the treaty on Danube navigation of 7 November 1857 enacted by the above-mentioned laws;   
b) to abide by the provisions of the "provisional regulations on navigation on rivers, canals, and lakes" issued by 
   the former minister of public works and traffic, number 2362 of 1869, as well as the provisions of Article XXXVII of 
   1875 and Article II of 1890 on public roads and customs, as well as Article II of 1873 on the employment of 
   discharged non-commissioned officers;   
c) to abide by all laws already in force, or that will later be enacted, as well as all ministerial and other orders 
   of the authorities concerning public safety, public traffic, public and veterinary health, and fee provisions.   
   
§ 3 This activity may be conducted only with such steamboats that are provided with a boiler gauging certificate, as well 
as a ship's certificate issued on the basis thereof, under the guidance and care of a certified boatmaster and certified engineer.   

§ 4 On the basis of this permit and in the sense of § 2 of the agreement on Danube navigation of 1857, existing port rights 
may not be violated, wherefore the permit holder is required to unconditionally submit to the payment of fees secured 
by port patents, and insofar as it concerns port rights regarding the exercise of navigation, the regulation of these 
legal relations must precede the opening of the navigation business.   

§ 5 The permit holder is required to actually commence his steam navigation operation within one year counting from the 
issuance of this permit, and after that to carry it out regularly. Insofar as actual commencement of the operation is 
impossible within that time, the permit holder is required to request its extension from the Royal Hungarian 
Ministry of Commerce with indication of the intervening preventing circumstances.   

§ 6 For the actual commencement of the steam navigation operation, a special permit is required, which must be requested 
from the Royal Hungarian Ministry of Commerce through the Royal Hungarian General Inspectorate for Railways and 
Shipping, with simultaneous proof of exact adherence to and execution of the provisions contained in § 2 of the 
provisional regulations on river, canal, and lake navigation, No. 2362 of 21 April 1869. The Trade Register will issue 
a decision on granting the permit according to the result of any ordered inspection on the spot.   

§ 7 The permit holder is required to procure the transport equipment and materials used in his operation as far 
as possible within the country, and to see to it that at least two-thirds of his employees are Hungarian subjects.   

§ 8 The duration of this permit is not limited. The permit terminates, or the Minister may at any time revoke and cancel it, 
particularly:  
1. if the permit holder does not comply with the provisions of this document, particularly those contained in § 5;   
2. if the permit holder sells all his steamboats, in which case this permit must be sent for cancellation without any summons to 
the Royal Hungarian Ministry of Commerce, since it is issued in a name and cannot be transferred to another.   

§ 9 This permit cannot stand in the way of any state regulations regarding navigation, and in this respect gives 
the permit holder no right to legal claims.  

§  10. This permit is subject to a fixed fee stamp of 1 florin.

In Budapest, July 1, 1898

By order of the Minister: Illegible signature, m. p., Ministerial Councillor
