Hungarian Legal Introduction

    Notarial Deed

    Before me, Dr. Fülöp Weinmann, Royal Public Notary in Budapest, appeared the following clients, personally known to me:

        I. Guttmann, wholesale merchant residing locally at Arany János utca 20;

        Traian Mebius, landowner residing in Terezinstadt;

        Jacob Haas and Ludwig Deutsch, wholesale merchants residing locally at 2 Gyár Street;

    ...and the clients requested that I draft and notarize the following agreement:

German Contract Text

    1. Between the named parties, a limited partnership (Kommanditgesellschaft) is hereby established under the laws 
	in force in the Kingdom of Romania.

    As personally liable general partners (Komplementäre), the gentlemen I. Guttmann and Traian Mebius shall act; as 
	limited partners (Kommanditisten), the gentlemen Jacob Haas and Ludwig Deutsch.

    The firm name of the limited partnership shall be:

    "Holzindustrie Kommanditgesellschaft Mebius, Guttmann & Co."

    and shall be registered as a commercial entity in accordance with the laws in force in the Kingdom of Romania.

    The representation of the firm shall be carried out collectively by I. Guttmann or Traian Mebius together 
	with Ferdinand Neufeld as procurator (Prokurist), or jointly with another procurator appointed in his place...

    ...or other procurators.

    Object of the Limited Partnership is:

    a) The exploitation of timber rights on the forests located on the estate of M. Valima in the Kingdom of 
	Romania, under plot numbers 1787/1891, 1117/1894, bought under purchase contracts from G. Vasiu, provided 
	that the payment terms and other terms of these purchase contracts are strictly observed...

    b) The logging rights to the forests belonging to Traian Mebius in the Kingdom of Romania at Lăpușna 
	in the district of Bistrița-Năsăud, which he acquired through various purchase contracts (recorded 
	under Nos. 892/1893, 798/1893, 799/1893, 1089/1895, and 902/1895), on the condition that the limited 
	partnership assumes the remaining payment obligations for these logging rights, which shall not 
	be credited as capital contributions.

    Mr. Traian Mebius obligates himself to procure all necessary Romanian court authentications, 
	transfers, and land register entries for these forest rights without delay.

Page 3

    ...and Mr. Mebius shall transfer physical possession of the forests to the partnership immediately 
	so that logging operations can begin and continue unhindered by third parties.

    2. I. Guttmann contributes a capital investment of 60,000 (sixty thousand) Florins in cash. Jacob Haas 
	and Ludwig Deutsch jointly contribute a capital investment of 20,000 (twenty thousand) Florins in cash.

    Profits and capital contributions shall be distributed as follows: I. Guttmann shall hold a 4/5 
	(four-fifths) share, while Jacob Haas and Ludwig Deutsch together shall hold a 1/5 (one-fifth) share.

    Regarding the 340,000 (three hundred forty thousand) Florins in capital contributed by Jacob Haas 
	and Ludwig Deutsch, bills of exchange (Wechsel) shall be issued by the partnership from case to 
	case with a term of four months...

    ...and the bills of exchange shall be renewed upon maturity.

    These bills of exchange shall be considered administrative assets (Regieanlagen) of the partnership.

    3. Mr. Traian Mebius shall receive from the company a fee/royalty per cubic meter of timber harvested 
	from the Lăpușna forests, up to a total maximum amount...

    This royalty shall be credited to Mr. Traian Mebius's capital account.

    4. The commercial management and general administrative matters of the enterprise shall be conducted by I. Guttmann.

    All technical matters, forest exploitation, and logging operations shall be overseen by Traian Mebius.

    The management of office matters, accounting, and cashier operations shall 
	be entrusted to Ferdinand Neufeld as procurator.

Page 5

    Procurators may only act jointly with Jacob Haas or Ludwig Deutsch, or under their supervision.

    The partners Jacob Haas and Ludwig Deutsch are authorized to inspect the company's 
	premises, sawmills, accounts, and business books at any time.

    Regarding cash balances: It is agreed that the cash on hand shall not exceed 10,000 Florins. 
	Any amount exceeding 10,000 Florins shall be deposited with Jacob Haas and Ludwig Deutsch at interest.

    5. Financial statements and balance sheets shall be prepared annually as of December 31...

    ...and submitted to the partners Jacob Haas and Ludwig Deutsch.

    Net profit shall be calculated after deducting operational expenses, administrative costs, and depreciation.

    6. Net profits shall be distributed as follows:

        I. Guttmann: 1/3 (one-third)

        Traian Mebius: 1/3 (one-third)

        Jacob Haas & Ludwig Deutsch (jointly): 1/3 (one-third)

    Profits shall be credited to each partner's capital account and cannot be 
	withdrawn except as provided hereafter:

    a) First, all contractual liabilities to G. Vasiu for timber purchases must be fully satisfied.
    b) Second, capital contributions made by individual partners shall be repaid.

Page 7

    7. Once all purchase obligations and capital repayments are completed, partners may freely 
	dispose of their share of net profits.

    If a partner wishes to leave the company after full capital settlement, they may do so at 
	the end of any business year with prior notice.

    8. This contract is concluded for a fixed period of 6 (six) years, commencing on December 1, 1894, 
	and ending on November 30, 1900.

📄 Image 5: Pages 8 & 9
Page 8

    In the event of the death of I. Guttmann during the contract term, his sons shall step into 
	his rights and obligations.

    In the event of the death of Traian Mebius, his heirs shall step into his position in the partnership.

    Upon expiration of the contract term (November 30, 1900), liquidation of the limited partnership 
	shall take place in accordance with Romanian law.

Page 9

    9. Partners are strictly prohibited from engaging in any competing timber or forest business in the 
	Kingdom of Romania during the term of this contract, either directly or indirectly.

    10. All expenses, taxes, and legal costs incurred in connection with the creation and execution 
	of this contract shall be borne as general business expenses by the partnership.

    11. Any disputes arising from this contract or partnership relations shall be submitted exclusively 
	to the Court of Arbitration of the Budapest Goods and Securities Exchange (Budapesti Áru- és Értéktőzsde Schiedsgericht).
