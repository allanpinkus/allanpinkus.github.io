

Case No.: 1407 / 1891
NOTARIAL DEED.

Before me, Béla Gásznár, Royal Public Notary in Budapest, appeared the following parties:

Zsigmond Olászy, landowner residing in Temes-Krassó County, whose identity was verified for me by 
Dr. Vilmos Wolf, attorney residing in Budapest, and János Nemes, estate manager residing in Szerep—witnesses 
known to me personally; and furthermore Izsák Guttmann, factory/property owner residing at Gyár-utca 8, 6th 
District of Budapest, known to me personally; and the parties concluded the following contract:

—Inheritance Sale Contract—

1. Zsigmond Olászy hereby sells and transfers the following real estate properties owned by him:

a) The real estate registered under land registry unit No. 596 of Lomnesz, parcel numbers 3, 9, 22, 128, 130, 260, 261, 22, 264;

b) The real estate registered under land registry unit No. 504 of Lomnesz, parcel numbers 4, 7, 129, 263, 270, 261;

c) The real estate registered under land registry unit No. 6 of Lomnesz, parcel numbers 1, 3, together with its appurtenances;

d) The real estate registered under land registry unit No. 9 of Lomnesz, parcel numbers 1, 2, 5, 6, 7, 12, 129, 260, 262, 263, 
   along with all appurtenances and real property rights, to Izsák Guttmann, who buys and acquires them for the agreed purchase price.

2. The subject of this sale does NOT include: the courtyard, house, and outbuildings registered under land registry unit No. 584 of Lornese, 
   parcel numbers 8 and 10; nor the outbuildings registered under land registry unit No. 24, parcel number 16, which remain the exclusive property of Zsigmond Olászy.

3. Zsigmond Olászy surrenders to Izsák Guttmann the right of possession to the residential building situated on parcel No. 1, along with 
   the outbuildings belonging to it. The brick barn located on the boundary of parcel No. 1, which opens toward the courtyard of Zsigmond Olászy, 
   shall be divided by a wall. The seller shall ensure that the roof structure and fencing are completed so that Izsák Guttmann can take unhindered 
   possession of his portion by early November 1896.

4. Izsák Guttmann undertakes to surrender 80 Hungarian hold [~113 acres] of oak forest from his estate to Zsigmond Olászy in exchange for forest land. 
   Zsigmond Olászy shall clear this timber at his own expense within 3 years.

5. Zsigmond Olászy retains the right to graze his livestock on the unsold pastures.

6. Zsigmond Olászy retains the right to use the well located on the property purchased by Izsák Guttmann for watering his livestock.

7. The agreed purchase price for the sold properties is 88,000 (eighty-eight thousand) Hungarian florins (Gulden).

    Upon signing this contract, Izsák Guttmann pays 38,000 florins directly to Zsigmond Olászy, who hereby acknowledges receipt.

    The remaining balance of 50,000 florins shall be paid by Izsák Guttmann to Zsigmond Olászy by early November 1891.

8. Zsigmond Olászy guarantees that the properties sold are entirely free and clear of all encumbrances, taxes, or claims. Zsigmond Olászy 
   consents to the immediate registration of Izsák Guttmann's ownership right in the land register. The remaining purchase price balance 
   shall accrue interest at a rate of 5.5% per annum until paid.

9. Izsák Guttmann shall enter into actual physical possession and enjoyment of the lands in early November 1891, from which date he 
   shall also bear all taxes and public burdens.

10. Zsigmond Olászy warrants and guarantees full legal title for the sold real estate properties to Izsák Guttmann.

11. Zsigmond Olászy retains the right to repurchase up to 30 Hungarian hold of land from the property if Izsák Guttmann does not fulfill 
    certain payment conditions by early November.

12. All legal fees, notary fees, and land registry transfer costs associated with this transaction shall be borne by the buyer, Izsák Guttmann.

13. Any legal disputes arising from this contract shall fall under the jurisdiction of the competent court in Budapest.

14. Zsigmond Olászy warrants that the real estate is fully operational and maintained in good condition.

This contract was read aloud to the parties, who declared it to be in full accordance with their intentions and signed it in person.

Done in Budapest, on the fourteenth (14th) day of May in the year eighteen hundred and ninety-one (1891).

Signatures & Seals:

    Zsigmond Olászy (Seller)

    Izsák Guttmann (Buyer)

    Dr. Vilmos Wolf (Witness)

    János Nemes (Witness)

    Béla Gásznár (Royal Public Notary)
