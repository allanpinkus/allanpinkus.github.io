


1406 / 1894 Notarial Document

Before me, Dr. Béla Fischer, Royal Public Notary in Budapest, appeared today the following parties, 
namely:Mrs. Géza Virányi, born Róza Szöbé, widow of the late Adolf Káztics, landowner residing in Szegény, 
whose identity was confirmed to me by the witnesses Dr. Vilmos Wolf, lawyer in Budapest, and Sándor Weisz, 
merchant in Magyargencs, both known to me personally; furthermore Mr. Izsák Guttmann, house owner residing 
in Budapest, of legal age, fully capable of acting; and the parties concluded the following contract:

Contract of Perpetual Sale (Örökeladási szerződés)

1. Mrs. Adolf Káztics, born Róza Szöbé, with the consent of her current husband Géza Virányi, sells in perpetuity 
and transfers into the ownership of the buyer the following real estate properties: 

a) The real estates registered in land register ledger No. 108 under topographical numbers $A \dagger 1-10$, $\frac{2}{a}$, 
$15$, $\frac{20}{b}$, $112$, $124$, $\frac{360}{a}$, $367$, $368$, $19$, $21$.

b) The real estates registered in land register ledger No. 118 under topographical numbers $A \dagger 1-5$, 
$\frac{2}{a}$, $\frac{20}{b}$, $a 17$.

c) The real estate portion listed under $97.3$ out of the real estate registered 
in land register ledger No. 6 under topographical number $A \dagger 50n 6$.

d) The real estate out of the properties registered in land register ledger No. 9 under topographical 
numbers $A \dagger 1, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 13, 127, \frac{266}{a}, \frac{268}{a}, 369, 12b$, corresponding 
to $A 19$, as well as all rights, appurtenances, or shares belonging to these real estates, fully and 
without any reservation, as they are presently situated, without any exception, to Mr. Izsák Guttmann, 
who hereby purchases these real estates and real estate portions for himself.

The purchase price is established and mutually agreed upon at 28,000 fl. (twenty-eight thousand florins) 
in Austrian currency, payable as follows:

a) Upon the signing of the present contract, the buyer hands over to the seller the sum of 12,000 fl. 
(twelve thousand florins) as down payment, the receipt of which is hereby acknowledged and confirmed by the seller.

b) The remaining balance of 16,000 fl. (sixteen thousand florins) shall be paid by the buyer to the 
seller on November 1 of the current year, together with 5% (five percent) interest accrued from today's date. 
If upon payment the sold real estates are completely unencumbered, or if any existing tax arrears or stamp duty 
debts are deducted from the purchase price, then the seller agrees that title to the properties be registered 
in favor of Mr. Izsák Guttmann.

The buyer shall take possession of the real estates on November 1 of the current year; however, if the real estates 
are not completely unencumbered, but encumbrances exceed the remaining purchase price, then Mr. Izsák Guttmann 
shall not be obliged to pay the purchase price balance and interest to the seller, but rather to pay off the 
encumbrances at the Royal Public Notary in Budapest, who is hereby instructed to satisfy the claims 
from the balance and any surplus shall be handed over to the seller. However, if the encumbrances exceed 
the remaining purchase price balance, Mr. Izsák Guttmann is entitled to demand compensation from the seller 
for any amount paid above the purchase price balance.

3. The seller guarantees that the sold real estates shall pass into the buyer's ownership completely free 
of all encumbrances, claims, or liabilities.

4. The buyer shall enter into possession and use of the real 
estates on November 1 of the current year, and from that day forward shall bear all burdens and taxes. 
Consequently, all income and yields up to November 1 of the current year belong to the seller, and the seller 
shall likewise bear all burdens up to that day. The seller reserves the right to cut and harvest the 
timber in the forest.

5. Mrs. Adolf Káztics, born Róza Szöbé, with the consent of her husband Géza Virányi consents that 
Mr. Izsák Guttmann may pre-register (előjegyeztesse) or, upon full payment or deposit of the remaining 
purchase price, permanently register (bekebeleztesse) his ownership right to the real estates mentioned in point 1.

6. All costs, duties, land registry registration fees, transfer fees, and cancellation fees arising 
from this deed shall be borne entirely by the buyer.

7. For the settlement of any legal disputes arising from this transaction, the parties submit themselves 
to the jurisdiction and simplified procedure of the relevant Budapest competent district court.

This deed was read aloud to the parties, explained, found correct in every respect, approved, and signed by hand.

Executed in Budapest, on May 4, 1894 (eighteen hundred and ninety-four).

(Signatures & Seals)

Mrs. Géza Virányi, born Róza Szöbé (Seller)Géza Virányi (Husband giving consent)

Izsák Guttmann (Buyer)

Dr. Vilmos Wolf (Witness)
Sándor Weisz (Witness)
Dr. Béla Fischer (Royal Public Notary seal & signature) 

Single copy issued to Izsák Guttmann on May 10, 1894, under No. 271/894.(Official notary signature)
