PARTNERSHIP CONTRACT

Concluded on this day between Lipót Frank, resident of Újvidék, Alfréd Guttmann, and Hermann Guttmann, residents of Budapest, under the following terms:   

1. The named parties unite today for the purpose of carrying on a construction enterprise and another business to be 
specified below under the registered firm name of Guttmann and Frank.   

2. The purpose and object of the general partnership established on this basis is that the commercial enterprise 
hitherto carried on by Izsák Guttmann and Lipót Frank in connection with the Kroden stone quarries shall henceforth 
be continued under the named firm, and that based on the lease agreement concluded simultaneously, they shall 
utilize the Kredin [Krčedin] stone quarries to the greatest possible extent.   

3. Inasmuch as the contracting parties operated under the name of Izsák Guttmann and Lipót Frank, and Izsák Guttmann 
transferred his rights in those enterprises and in all of them to the contracting parties, they consider it their 
primary duty to effect a final settlement with Izsák Guttmann, which final settlement and balance sheet shall represent 
not only the rights and obligations of Izsák Guttmann, but also the initial inventory of the firm now created.   

Those lease agreements by which Lipót Frank leased stone quarries in the villages of Kamenitz, Ledincze, Rakovácz, 
Cserevics, and Bánostor shall pass as of today to the account of the partner firm, and the lease payments shall also be payable at its expense.   

[According to Gemini, Kamenitz (today Sremska Kamenica (Szerémkamenic)) is located directly across the Danube River from Novi Sad. 
Today, it is an urban settlement within the City Municipality of Petrovaradin. Ledincze (today Ledinci / Stari Ledinci (Ledince))
is a village situated just west of Sremska Kamenica along the northern foot of Fruška Gora. Rakovácz (today Rakovac (Rakovác))
is located just west of Ledinci in the municipality of Beočin. Historically famous for the 15th-century Rakovac Monastery and its nearby stone quarries.
Cserevics(today Čerević (Cserevics)) is a Danube-side village in the municipality of Beočin, further west along the river road.
While Bánostor (today Banoštor (Bánmonostor)) is located a few kilometers west of Čerević on the Danube, near the border of the 
Beočin municipality. All five of these locations are historical towns and villages situated along the right bank of the Danube River, 
along the northern slopes of the Fruška Gora mountain range. This stretch of the Danube shoreline at the foot of Fruška Gora 
was historically rich in trachytic stone and trachyte-basalt quarries (often referred to in 19th and early 
20th-century commercial records as the Krcedin / Rakovac stone quarries). Building materials from these quarries were transported 
down the Danube River directly to major urban construction sites in Újvidék (Novi Sad) and Budapest, making them valuable 
leaseholds for industrial and construction partnerships like the one described in the 1903 contract. AP]

4. The contracting parties shall share equally in profits and losses, and bind themselves to serve the common interests 
of the company members with all possible diligence and commercial care.   

Each member of the firm is authorized to sign for the firm. It is mutually stipulated by the firm members that all enterprises 
to be undertaken by individual members either in the name of the firm, or in their own name, or in joint enterprise 
with others or under others' names, shall be placed at the disposal of the firm, provided that the execution of these 
enterprises or business transactions has been decided in advance by the majority of the members, or approved 
retroactively, as if contracted by the firm itself.   

5. As business capital, the individual members contribute equally, primarily with the asset that accrues to them from the 
business acquired from Izsák Guttmann and Lipót Frank, and bind themselves to supplement their respective entitlements 
shown in this initial inventory up to 100,000 Kronen each.   

6. The duration of the present partnership relation is fixed for 10 (ten) years in such a manner that, if no notice 
of termination is given among the firm members six months prior to September 1, 1913, this partnership relation 
shall be deemed tacitly extended until notice is given by any of the firm members.   

Upon expiration of the contract term or following notice of termination, the partnership shall enter liquidation. 
If the parties cannot reach an agreement regarding the method of liquidation, the liquidation methods 
prescribed by law shall apply.   

7. If any of the firm members should pass away prior to the expiration of the present contract, the present contractual 
relationship shall nevertheless remain unchanged, and the heirs of the deceased partner shall participate in all 
benefits to which the deceased partner was entitled.   


Page 7

8. The seat of the firm shall be in Újvidék, where the firm's registration and bookkeeping shall be conducted; however, 
detailed monthly extracts of this bookkeeping shall be submitted to the Budapest-registered firm of I. Guttmann and Sons, 
so that the status of all transactions and the management of the business may be known at the newly named head office.   

9. A balance sheet shall be prepared at the end of each calendar year. Individual partners are entitled to dispose of profit 
shown to their credit as far as possible; in case of loss, however, they are obliged to make up the deficit to the extent 
of their capital contribution specified in this agreement.   

10. Each individual partner is entitled to draw in advance from the partnership treasury for his own household purposes 
a monthly sum of 1000 K (One Thousand Kronen), which amount shall naturally be charged to the respective partner's account.   

Profit bonuses not drawn by individual partners shall be credited on account of their capital, and the firm shall pay 
6% interest per annum on such capital.   

11. In consideration of the circumstance that Lipót Frank has shared to a greater extent in the joint enterprises 
conducted hitherto, whereas contracting partners Alfréd Guttmann and Hermann Guttmann were each 33 1/3% owners in the 
coal business of the Budapest registered firm I. Guttmann and Sons, as well as in the Ráthonyi coal mine acquired by them, 
they hereby declare that their participation in the firm as well as their 11% (Eleven percent) share each in the Ráthonyi 
coal mine is assigned to Lipót Frank, while the latter's participation in the coal business of I. Guttmann and Sons as well 
as a 22% share in the Ráthonyi coal mine is transferred to them.   


Page 8

Frank Lipót accepts this participation in accordance with the offer and for the duration of the present contract, 
and binds himself to fulfill his obligations in proportion to the commissions of these companies or enterprises.   

The parties note that it is their intention to publicly register and regulate the participation contained in this clause.   

[According to Gemini the Ráthonyi Coal Mine (Ráthonyi-kőszénbánya or Ráthonyi-bányatelep) was a prominent brown coal 
(lignite) mining concession located in Baglyasalja (today a district of Salgótarján), within Nógrád County, Hungary. AP]

12. Regarding any litigation arising from this contract, the parties submit themselves to the jurisdiction of the 
Budapest Commercial and Stock Exchange Court.   

In witness whereof, three identical copies of this contract were drawn up, read aloud before the parties, explained, 
and signed in the presence of two requested witnesses.   


Budapest, August 25, 1903.
