


Case No.: 790/1894
NOTARIAL DEED.

Before me, DR. KÁROLY WEISER, Royal Public Notary in Budapest,

at the place, year, month, and day written below, appeared the following parties 
known to me personally: Ignác Guttmann, building contractor, and his wife Katalin Leinkauf, private individual, 
residing in Budapest, District X, in their own home at Szegyház Street; further, as the 
minors' natural guardian and representative, the child's mother—

and declared the following—

—contract—
Adoption Contract.

Ignác Guttmann, born in the village of Réce, Pozsony County, born in the month of October 1840, by virtue of his present 
declaration, hereby adopts as his own children, with full inheritance rights, the minor children born to his wife, the 
above-mentioned Katalin Leinkauf, widow of the late Miklós Eisner: Ármin Eisner (Hermán), born in Vienna on March 18, 1882, 
and István Eisner (István), born in Vienna on February 19, 1886, who are under guardianship—
gated present declaration, declares that he considers Ármin Eisner and Aladár [István] as his own minor children, 
to be loved and cared for, and promises to treat them in every respect as his own children, and to accept 
them as heirs to his estate on equal terms.

    Ármin Eisner and Aladár, through their mother as their natural guardian, gratefully and appreciatively 
	accept this offer made in their favor, and furthermore, under the name Guttmann Ármin and Aladár, adopt and 
	accept the adopter as their father, acknowledging their filial duties toward the adoptive father during his 
        lifetime and after his death.

    Katalin Leinkauf, wife of Ignác Guttmann, takes note of the offer made in favor of her children and the 
        declaration of adoption, and obligates herself to fully respect and support her husband's act of love and goodwill.

    The contracting parties reserve the right to secure the validity of this adoption through the competent judicial authorities.

Which deed, having been read aloud to the parties present, translated and explained to them in the German language, was approved by them 
in all respects as correct, and signed by them with their own hands—the line-by-line insertions being noted as one word—in Budapest, 
on the twenty-fourth day of May in the year one thousand eight hundred and ninety-four.

Signatures:

    Güttman Ignácz

    Eisner Ármin

    Eisner Aladár

    Katalin Güttman born Leinkauf

Note: The left column also contains official notary fee stamps and a signature by the notary's clerk, "Krmenitzky". Page 3 (on the right) 
contains a notary's archival note and index reference regarding the case file.
