


Mutual Will.

We, the undersigned spouses, have on the date set below, while in full consciousness and undisturbed mental capacity, 
by mutual consent, made the following last disposition:

Since at the time of our marriage neither of us possessed independent assets, everything we currently own 
is consequently to be regarded as our jointly acquired property.

Upon executing this last disposition, we mutually undertake the obligation that if one or the other of us 
should pass away earlier, the surviving party cannot and may not alter the dispositions made herein, 
and these shall be carried out unaltered in the manner determined herein.

I. If one of us should die earlier, all of our jointly acquired property shall pass into the unrestricted 
possession of the surviving party, and we accordingly mutually appoint the surviving party as the universal heir of our entire estate.

II. Since each of us already had two living children from their first marriage, and two living children also originate 
from our joint marriage, we stipulate that when the surviving party of us also passes away in death, our children, namely:

    Johanna, married to Leopold Frank,

    Hermine, married to Goldstein,
    both from the first marriage of Isaak Guttmann;

    Alfred (Eisner) Guttmann,
    Hermann (Eisner) Guttmann,
    both from the first marriage of Mrs. Isaak Guttmann, with Max Eisner;

    Berta, married to Hermann Zweibel,
    Malvine, married to Julius Bottenstein,
    originating from the joint marriage of the spouses disposing herein;
   
shall inherit the entire left-behind property as our universal heirs—without regard to their lineage—in equal shares.

III. Should one of the children listed in the previous point, or their descendants, contest the joint disposition made 
herein—specifically the provision that after appointing each other as universal heirs, the succession of our children 
shall only occur in the event of the death of the surviving party—for this case (which is unlikely to occur), 
we stipulate that said child or their successors shall inherit only a statutory share (Pflichtteil), that is to say, 
receive only half of that inheritance which would otherwise fall to them.

IV. In consideration of the circumstance that our joint daughters Berta and Malvine, despite their marriage, are financially 
not in as good a position as our other children, we stipulate that both Berta and Malvine shall each receive 50,000 K., 
that is, fifty thousand Kronen, as a special pre-legacy, specifically in such a manner that these amounts shall be satisfied 
before all others, and only the remaining property shall form the subject of the division of inheritance.

V. We determine the following legacies:

1) On the day of the funeral of each one of us, 2,000 ,two thousand Kronen, shall be distributed to the poor 
and needy in the house of death itself.

The sisters of Mr. Issak Guttmann:

2) Johanna Grotter, née Guttmann, residing in Szempen;
3) Betty Steiner, née Guttmann, residing in Boldogkő;
4) Antonie Wilhelm, née Guttmann, residing in Fodernes; and
5) Heinrich Guttmann, brother of Mr. Issak Guttmann, residing in Rete;

shall receive, upon the death of Mr. Issak Guttmann, 2,000 two thousand Kronen each.

6) Hedwig Rosenthal,
7) Henriette Rosenthal, and
8) Camilla Rosenthal,

children of the sister of Mr. Issak Guttmann, residing in Szempen, shall likewise receive 2,000, two thousand Kronen, 
each toward their dowry.

9) Rosa Kohn, residing in Budapest (Vörösmarty utcza 12);
10) Widow Betty Wolf, residing in Vienna;

both as children of the sister of the testatrix, Mrs. Issak Guttmann, shall receive 2,000, two thousand Kronen each.

11) The "Torah" owned by the testators, which is currently located in Rete, is bequeathed by the testators to their son-in-law, 
Mr. Leopold Frank, with the provision that this "Torah" shall initially remain in use by the community of Rete, but then be 
taken over by Mr. Leopold Frank and kept in the Novi Sad Israelite temple.
12) We further bequeath 1,000, one thousand Kronen, to the synagogue construction fund of the Budapest Israelite Community;
13) 400, four hundred Kronen, to the Israelite Boys' Orphanage of Budapest;
14) 400, four hundred Kronen, to the Israelite Girls' Orphanage of Budapest;
15) 400, four hundred Kronen, to the Israelite Craftsmen's Association of Budapest;
16) 400, four hundred Kronen, to the Budapest Educational Association.

The legacies listed here, namely from 1–8 and from 11–16, shall be paid out after the death of Mr. Issák Guttmann, 
while legacies 1, 9, and 10 shall be paid out after the death of Mrs. Issak Guttmann.

The testators executing this document express their mutual expectation that the surviving party will continue to support 
those poor relatives, poor individuals, and institutions, which they have jointly supported up until now, at their own discretion, 
just as they have done up until now.

VI. Since on various occasions, including upon the marriage of all our children, we provided them with dowries and various forms of 
support, we wish that all these gifts made up until our death shall be disregarded during the distribution of the estate, and that 
the inheritance be divided in equal shares without regard to the parts of assets already received.

It goes without saying, however, and we wish to emphasize explicitly here, that the mutual claims arising from business 
relationships that are still outstanding, which are evident from the account books maintained at the Budapest firm "I. Guttmann & Söhne," 
remain untouched by this disposition.

We wish and hope that our beloved children will faithfully and honorably fulfill all the dispositions listed herein.

And now we commend our souls to the mercy of the Almighty and pray for the blessing upon the heads of our dearly beloved children.
Amen!

In witness whereof, we have executed this joint will today and, after reading it through, have signed it with our own hands 
in the presence of the witnesses requested to attend.

Budapest, on the 31st day of August, 1903.

Issak Guttmann

Katarina Guttmann, nee Leinkauf


(Note: The lower portion and right page transition into Hungarian legal attestations by notary witnesses 
and court officials dated January 9, 1906, acknowledging receipt and presentation of the joint will).

My Remarks: 1 Krone is about 8 US dollars in modern consumer purchasing power.

Contextualizing the Bequests in the Will:

2,000 Kronen (bequeathed to siblings and charities): Equal to roughly 1.5 to 2 years of full income for a skilled worker or lower civil servant.

50,000 Kronen (bequeathed to daughters Berta and Malvine): Equivalent to roughly 35 to 50 years of an average worker's annual income, or over 5 years' earnings for a top civil servant or judge.

Rete is the Hungariqan form of Reca where Issak Guttmann was born. 

I do not know the women mentioned in 9 and 10. (Perhaps related to Max Eisner?)
