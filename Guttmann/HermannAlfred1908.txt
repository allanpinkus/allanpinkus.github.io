


Case No.: 40 / 1908

Notarial Document

Before me, Béla Gáspárdy, Royal Notary of Budapest, appeared persons known to me personally: Alfréd Guttmann 
(residing at Podmaniczky Street No. 43) and Herman Guttmann (residing at V. Alsó rakpart No. 4), residents of 
Budapest, who are joint members entitled to represent and sign on behalf of the registered Budapest firm 
"Guttmann F. és Fiai" (F. Guttmann and Sons), and made and issued before me the following:

Declaration and Power of Attorney

1. We, the undersigned Alfréd Guttmann and Herman Guttmann, as sole members of the registered Budapest 
firm "Guttmann F. és fiai", hereby declare that the mentioned firm is being dissolved via simplified 
liquidation, and consequently, upon termination, the registered firm shall be deleted from the trade register.

2. With reference to the provision in point 1, the sole members of the firm "Guttmann F. és fiai", namely 
the undersigned Alfréd Guttmann and Herman Guttmann, hereby unconditionally and irrevocably appoint and 
empower each other—undersigned Alfréd Guttmann and Herman Guttmann separately—to collect and receive any 
postal items or money orders, as well as ordinary and registered letters, postcard letters, and postal money 
orders addressed to the currently existing but soon-to-be-dissolved registered Budapest firm "Guttmann F. 
és fiai" or to "Dr. Mártus I.B.", either separately during the existence of the firm or after its dissolution, 
thus one without the other, and to legally acknowledge receipt thereof.

3. Certified copies of this document may be issued in unlimited number to Alfréd Guttmann and Herman Guttmann.

After this notary document was read aloud to the appearing parties, they approved it as fully 
matching their intent in every respect, and signed it below in their own hands together 
with me, the notary.

Done at Budapest, December 7, 1908 (nineteen hundred and eight).

(Signatures)

Guttmann F. és Fiai

Guttmann Alfréd

Guttmann Herman

(Notary seal and signature of Béla Gáspárdy)
