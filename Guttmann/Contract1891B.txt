

Case No.: 183 / 1891
NOTARIAL DEED.

Before me, Dénes Salamon, Royal Public Notary,

appeared Izsák Guttmann, building contractor, resident of Novi Sad, known to me personally, as owner of the firm "I. Guttmann", and issued 
the following declaration for József Weisz, site engineer, resident of Ada:

—Power of Attorney.—

"In consideration of the fact that I, Izsák Guttmann, withdrew my power of attorney granted to site engineer Ferenc 
Stein before this notary's office under case No. 380/91 on July 7, 1891, I, Izsák Guttmann, as owner of the building 
contracting firm "I. Guttmann" existing in Novi Sad, hereby authorize József Weisz, site engineer, resident of Ada, 
to manage and direct on my behalf and as manager the earthworks undertaken for the Royal Ministry of Agriculture—namely 
section No. 93 at the Bácska embankment, section No. 94 at Ada-Mohol, and section No. 96 at the 
Bogojevo Tisza shortcut—accordingly authorizing him to make all necessary technical arrangements on my behalf, 
the results of which I acknowledge as my own.

This deed having been read aloud by me, the notary, to the party present, he declared his full agreement therewith and signed it."

Done in Zenta, on the fifteenth (15th) day of the month of October in the year eighteen hundred and ninety-one (1891).

Signatures & Stamps:

    J. Guttmann (Principal / Authorizing party)

    Dénes Salamon (Royal Public Notary)
