Case No.: 380 / 1891
NOTARIAL DEED.

I, Dénes Salamon, candidate notary public, acting as official deputy appointed by order No. 257/90 of the Szeged Royal Notarial 
Chamber for Antal Zsirkó, Royal Public Notary in Zenta,

hereby certify that there appeared before me Izsák Guttmann, building contractor residing in Novi Sad, as owner of the firm "I. Guttmann", 
whose personal identity was verified for me by Miksa Strausz and Benő Goldstein, merchants residing in Zenta, who are known to me 
personally; and in the presence of Ferenc Stein, site engineer residing in Adorján, he issued the following declaration:

—Power of Attorney—

"I, Izsák Guttmann, as owner of the building contracting firm "I. Guttmann" existing in Novi Sad, hereby authorize Ferenc Stein, site engineer residing in Adorján, to manage and direct on my behalf and as manager the earthworks undertaken for the Royal Ministry of Agriculture, namely section No. 93 at the Bácska embankment, section No. 94 at Ada-Mohol, and section No. 96 at the Bogojevo railway line; accordingly, I authorize him to make all necessary technical arrangements on my behalf, the results of which I acknowledge as my own.

This deed having been read aloud to the parties present by me, the notary's deputy, they declared their full agreement therewith and signed it."

Done in Zenta, on the seventh (7th) day of July in the year eighteen hundred and ninety-one (1891).

Signatures & Stamps:

    J. Guttmann (Principal / Authorizing party)

    Miksa Strausz (Identity witness)

    Benő Goldstein (Identity witness)

    Dénes Salamon (Notary Public Deputy)

(Official stamp of Antal Zsirkó, Royal Notary Public in Zenta, and purple official certification seal of the Notary Deputy).
