

This document is a Notarial Deed of a Real Estate Purchase & Sale Contract (Közjegyzői okirat / Örökeladási szerződés) 
written in Hungarian, drawn up in Budapest in November 1894 by Royal Public Notary Dr. Béla Gärtner.

The seller is Countess Irma Hugonnay acting as the legal guardian (gondnok) of her husband, Károly Zamoysky (written as Zamoysky Károly), 
and the buyer is Izsák Guttmann.

Verbatim Translation
Page 1

Case No.: 1405 / 1894

Notarial Deed

Before me, Dr. Béla Gärtner, Royal Public Notary of Budapest, appeared today the following parties known to me:

Countess Irma Hugonnay, wife of Károly Zamoysky, living in Liptó-Szent-Miklós, as well as the witnesses Dr. Vilmos Wolf, 
lawyer residing in Budapest, and Sándor Weisz, merchant residing in Megyer; furthermore, Izsák Guttmann, real estate 
owner residing in Budapest (District VIII, Práter Street 3), personally known to me and to the witnesses—and the 
parties concluded the following agreement:

Real Estate Purchase Contract (Örökeladási szerződés)

    Her Excellency Countess Irma Hugonnay, wife of Károly Zamoysky, as the legally appointed guardian (gondnok) of her husband, 
	Károly Zamoysky, placed under guardianship by the decision of the Royal Court of Budapest dated May 8, 1893 (No. 9439) sells 
	in perpetuity (örök áron eladja) to Izsák Guttmann, who buys in perpetuity, the real estate properties owned by Károly Zamoysky, namely:

    a) The real estate properties registered in the land register of Liptó-Szent-Miklós under topographical numbers 
	(helyrajzi szám) 1–12, 9, 14, 18, 92, 125, 389, 566, 577, 1/2 and 1/2, together with the house under No. 9.

    b) The real estate properties registered in the land register of Liptó-Szent-Miklós under topographical 
	numbers 1, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 127, 226, 227, 369, 127, consisting of 12/27 parts belonging to Károly Zamoysky, 
	1/6 part belonging to Károly Zamoysky Jr., and 1/6 part belonging to Károly Zamoysky, without any reservations or 
	conditions, with full warranty (szavatosság) to Izsák Guttmann, who hereby purchases them.

    The purchase price is fixed at 20,000 (Twenty Thousand) Florins (forint), payable as follows:

    a) Upon signing this document, 2,000 (Two Thousand) Florins is paid in cash to Countess Irma Hugonnay, the receipt of which she hereby acknowledges.

    b) The remaining balance of 18,000 (Eighteen Thousand) Florins shall be paid by the buyer at the beginning of November of the current year, 
	upon approval by the Guardianship Authority (gyámhatóság) and transfer of title to Countess Irma Hugonnay, provided that the sold real 
	estate properties are entirely clear of all encumbrances and liens...

    The buyer shall enter into possession and enjoyment of the property starting from November of the current year and shall be entitled to all 
	profits and revenues starting from the beginning of November of the current year.

    The seller guarantees that the sold real estate properties are entirely free of all debts, liens, and encumbrances.

    The seller shall pay interest at the rate of 3.5% (Three and a half percent) per annum on the remaining purchase price until payment 
	at the beginning of November of the current year.

    Countess Irma Hugonnay hereby consents that, upon approval of this contract by the Guardianship Authority, the ownership right of 
	Izsák Guttmann be registered in the land registry for the aforementioned properties.

    If approval of this agreement by the Guardianship Authority is not granted or obtained by November of the current year, this contract shall become 
	null and void, and the down payment of 2,000 Florins shall be returned to the buyer.

    All expenses, court fees, stamps, and costs of transfer of title associated with this transaction shall be borne by the buyer, Izsák Guttmann.

    For any legal disputes arising from this transaction, the parties submit themselves to the jurisdiction of the District Court of Budapest.

This deed was read aloud to the parties, accepted in its entirety, and signed before me.

Done at Budapest, November 1, 1894.

Countess Irma Hugonnay

as guardian of Károly Zamoysky

Izsák Guttmann (Buyer)

Dr. Vilmos Wolf (Witness)

Sándor Weisz (Witness)

(Official Notarial Seal of Dr. Béla Gärtner, Royal Public Notary, Budapest)
